The data behind the screens

One change unlocks most of the new UI

The app already reads every number it needs for day-level insight. It adds them up, keeps the total, and drops the rest on the floor.

Kept today
monthlyDigests[]
33 fields, one row per month. The canonical money record.
Kept today
config
Hardware, tariff schedule, plans, lease, counterfactual.
Kept today
chargingLog[]
One row per paid public charge, with a date.
Kept today
evChargingSessions[]
Real start and end timestamps. The only sub-daily source in the app.
Thrown away on every upload

Both monthly exports are already one row per day. parseFronius.js and parseWattpilot.js sum the columns, keep the totals plus one derived day count each, and discard every daily row. That is roughly 300 numbers a month, about 9,000 across the tracked period, read and dropped.
